# CoreWeather

### A pocket observatory for a restless sky

**[Download](releases/CoreWeather-1.3.0.apk)** · Android 11+ · no account · no ads

---

Five minutes to leave. Laundry on the line. Another app shows a cloud and a number. Will it rain? When? How much? That cloud does not know.

CoreWeather shows the city you are standing in. Not a placeholder. A chart from now, not from midnight. Rain as a story: when it starts, when it peaks, when it lets go.

On the map the rain moves. On the home screen a small window into the week. You do not have to open the app to know whether to take a coat.

The sky is a living system. This one listens.

---

Weather, radar, air, and the week in one place. Use location, or search for a city. The forecast stays on the phone. No account.

---

For a cyclist. For a parent with a pram. For anyone with laundry on the line and five minutes to decide.

This is not a station that measures the future. It is a window onto what is coming toward you.
