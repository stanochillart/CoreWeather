CoreWeather

A pocket observatory for a restless sky

Download · Android 11+ · no account · no ads

The app

Five minutes to leave. Laundry on the line. Another app shows a cloud and a number. Will it rain? When? How much? That cloud does not know.

CoreWeather shows the city you are standing in. A chart from now, not from midnight. Rain as a story: when it starts, when it peaks, when it lets go.

On the map the rain moves. On the home screen a small window into the week.

Weather, radar, air, and the week in one place. This is not a station that measures the future. It is a window onto what is coming toward you.
